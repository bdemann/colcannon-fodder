## Itch.io page text

### Tagline

Cook, cut and mash potatoes before the customers lose their patience.

### Pitch

Potatoes ride a conveyor belt toward your restaurant, and every one has to arrive **mashed**. Build
Knives, Boiling Pots and Mashers beside the belt, chain them into a **x3 SMASH!** combo, top the
mash by hand for customers' orders, and keep your restaurant happy until the last potato is served.
Earn the title of **Mash Champ**.

### Features

-   Seven towers: Knife (cuts straight across the belt), Boiling Pot, Masher, Microwave, Cricket Trap, Swiper and the late-game Laser (the aimable long line).
-   A cut x boil x mash combo with big floating hit numbers.
-   Six potato varieties with traits that change the plan. Tough skin: Cut it first. Dense: soften it first. Eyes: blades struggle.
-   Thrown projectiles: spinning mashers (meat hammers at level 3), boiling water and crickets fly
    from the towers. Purely visual.
-   Hand-applied toppings and customer orders: Buttered Mash, Champ, Colcannon, Bangers & Mash.
-   Potatoes are judged the instant they reach the restaurant: no queue, no second chances.
-   Thieving seagulls, tower wear and servicing, upgrades, and restaurant prestige: you start at zero stars with only the Knife, quality mash unlocks towers and upgrades, and poor potatoes (rubbery Microwave mash, raw ones) cost stars and lock them again.
-   Four maps, including the branching Fork, in Campaign (ending with the Russet King) or Endless.
-   Best scores and a Hall of Mash Champs, saved in your browser.

### Theme fit (Mashup)

Cooking game x tower defense: the towers are kitchen tools and the enemies are potatoes that need
to be turned into dinner. Everything is mashed together, including the potatoes. It also mashes up
the Irish and English plate: Champ and Colcannon sit next to Bangers & Mash, and the best you can
be is a Mash Champ.

### Controls

-   `1` to `7`: pick a tower. `Q` `W` `E` `R`: pick a topping (Butter, Scallions, Cabbage, Gravy).
-   Click: build a tower, top a potato, or select a tower. `U` / `S` / `X`: upgrade / service /
    sell the selected tower.
-   Scroll or `[` `]`: aim the Laser. Right click or `Esc`: cancel.
-   `P`: pause. `F`: fast-forward. `G`: fast-forward until the next income. `M`: mute.
-   `Space`: start. `R`: restart.

### How this game was made (AI disclosure)

The game concept, design direction and playtesting are by the human designer, bdemann.

**All code, art and documentation were produced by AI**: Claude (Anthropic) via Claude Code. Claude
Opus planned the game and wrote the specs as GitHub issues, and Claude Sonnet subagents implemented
them as pull requests. The designer never edited the code by hand.

All art is drawn in code (vector shapes). No AI image generation was used. The music is
`empacotatron_loop.ogg` by Fupi (CC0, from OpenGameArt), and the font is Fredoka (SIL Open Font
License, from Google Fonts). The game is built on the Antha engine by electrovir, starting from the
antha-template (MIT / CC0).

The full build log (issues and pull requests) is at https://github.com/bdemann/colcannon-fodder.
